What is Regular Value?

Regular (or Physical) value represents physical fruit items stored in your inventory. Prices fluctuate based on Beli cost, rarity, awakening potential, and current PvP meta.

What is Permanent Value?

Permanent value applies to fruits unlocked permanently using Robux. Since they can be equipped infinitely at any Blox Fruits dealer, their trading values are drastically higher.
